    Use case

    What do you do with $BLUB?

    You dress up Blub. In the Blub Shop you buy colors, eyes, hats and extras with $BLUB. Half of every purchase goes into the furnace, the other half into the Blub Jump weekly pot.

    1. 1Play1 coin = 5 BLUB

      In Blub Jump you hop over coral and grab coins. Whatever you grab goes into your wallet.

    2. 2Spend40 to 800 BLUB

      In the Blub Maker you buy items for your Blub. A crown costs 300, the diamond color 800.

    3. 3Burn50% into the furnace

      Half of every purchase is burned. That BLUB never comes back, so the supply shrinks.

    4. 4Win50% to the weekly pot

      The other half goes to the best jumpers. The weekly top 3 split the pot: 60, 30 and 10 percent.

    Why it works

    • You see where your BLUB went. Your crown stands in the Meadow for everyone to see.
    • Players pay players. The pot comes from the shop and goes to whoever jumps best.
    • Less BLUB in circulation. The more Blubs dress up, the more BLUB burns.
    • No two Blubs alike. With 20,160 combinations you make one that is yours alone.
